Wichita has three ways to get paper shredded: stores that weigh it and charge by the pound any day they are open, a commercial drop-off that charges by the tote for big loads, and free shred days hosted by banks and credit unions, mostly in spring and fall.

## When you have a few boxes and need it done now

Pay-by-the-pound drop-off is the quickest route. The UPS Store on W 21st St N says it works with Iron Mountain: your papers are weighed, you pay a per-pound price, and you put them in a locked shredding container. It accepts paper as is, with staples, paper clips, and folders, but not cardboard, phone books, hardcover books, CDs and DVDs, binders, hanging folders, transparencies, or large bulldog clips ([store page](https://locations.theupsstore.com/ks/wichita/8918-w-21st-st-n/shredding)).

Shred Nations lists The UPS Store at 6446 E Central Ave as its Wichita drop-off at $.99 per pound. It says drop-off is the economical choice under about 3 boxes (roughly 90 pounds), and that drop-off points don't let you watch the shredding or give a certificate of destruction ([Shred Nations Wichita](https://www.shrednations.com/wichita/)).

FedEx Office charges $1.49 per pound and says shredding is offered only at select stores. Its Wichita store pages for E Kellogg, N Rock Rd, S West St, and N Maize Rd all list shredding. FedEx says staples, paper clips, rubber bands, and small binder clips can stay on, but it won't take cardboard, books, 3-ring binders, hanging folders, or electronic media ([FedEx Office shredding](https://www.office.fedex.com/default/shredding-services)).

## When you are cleaning out a lot of paper

At $1 or more per pound, a big clean-out adds up fast. Shred-it lists a Wichita drop-off on W Pawnee St at $75 for a 96-gallon tote, which it says holds about 10 to 12 letter-size boxes. You need an appointment and should prepay online, since most locations can't take payment on site and cash isn't accepted ([Shred-it Wichita](https://www.shredit.com/en-us/service-locations/wichita)).

## When you can wait for a free event

Fall is shred season in Wichita. In 2026 INTRUST Bank ran free events at several Wichita-area branches from September through November 14, Heartland Credit Union held one at its East Kellogg branch on October 15, and Credit Union of America held members-only days at its Delano branch in April and September. Every one of those dates has passed. They are listed here so you know whose announcements to watch next year.

A local roundup, [Wichita on the Cheap](https://wichitaonthecheap.com/free-shredding-wichita-ks/), also lists past and member-only events from Wichita Federal Credit Union, nearby cities like Bel Aire and Park City (residents only), AARP, and others. Treat those as leads, not confirmed dates, and call to confirm.

Free events usually cap the number of boxes per car and run for just a few hours, so get there early.
