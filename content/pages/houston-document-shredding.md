Houston has no city-run shred program, so your options are monthly nonprofit shred days, donation events, and stores that charge by the pound. If you have just a box or two, some UPS Store and FedEx locations are cheap. For a big clean-out, look at the Pearland shred days or a Shred-it tote.

## Shred days coming up

**Keep Pearland Beautiful** runs a shred event almost every month at the Stella Roberts Recycling Center, 5800 Magnolia Pkwy in Pearland, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. The next dates are **Saturday, December 5, 2026**, January 9, 2027, and February 13, 2027 ([KPB events calendar](https://pearlandrecycles.com/calendar/)). KPB says its center serves residents from more than 30 cities. Members get 4 file boxes shredded free each month, and individual memberships start at $25 a year ([Join KPB](https://pearlandrecycles.com/join-kpb/)). KPB's event listing says non-members pay a $5 minimum or $0.30 a pound, whichever is higher ([event listing](https://events.yodel.today/pearland-tx/event/Keep-Pearland-Beautiful-/Paper-Shredding-Event/6a0fe2b72f1dea0f50240387)). Call (281) 489-2795 to confirm, and call ahead in bad weather, since outdoor events can be canceled.

**ECHOS**, a southwest Houston nonprofit, holds drive-up shred days at the Episcopal Church of the Epiphany, 9600 S Gessner Rd, from 9 a.m. to noon. The next one is **Saturday, February 6, 2027**, then May 1 and November 13, 2027. ECHOS asks for a minimum $15 donation for up to 5 boxes, and businesses are welcome too ([ECHOS Shred Days](https://echoshouston.org/shred-days/)).

## When you have a few boxes and need it done now

- **$1 a pound at two UPS Stores.** Shred Nations lists The UPS Store at 11152 Westheimer Rd and at 5535 Memorial Dr at $1 per pound, and says prices aren't guaranteed ([Westheimer](https://www.shrednations.com/tx/the-ups-store-16934-77042/), [Memorial Dr](https://www.shrednations.com/tx/the-ups-store-18326-77007/)).
- **FedEx Office, $1.09 to $1.56 a pound.** The FedEx inside Walmart at 3506 Hwy 6 S lists $1.09 ([store page](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/houston/office-2656)). Most Houston stores list $1.49, including Buffalo Speedway, Beechnut, Rice Blvd, Post Oak, Washington Ave, Westheimer, and Louisiana St downtown ([5110 Buffalo Speedway](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/houston/office-1034)). The convention-hotel stores at the Marriott Marquis and Hilton Americas list $1.56 ([1777 Walker St](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/houston/office-5705)). FedEx says staples, paper clips, and small binder clips can stay on ([FedEx Office shredding](https://www.office.fedex.com/default/shredding-services)).
- **The UPS Store.** Stores such as Downtown (945 McKinney) and Rice Village (2368A Rice Blvd) describe Iron Mountain drop-off: they weigh your paper, charge by the pound, and you put it in a locked bin. Their pages post no price, and prices vary by store ([945 McKinney](https://locations.theupsstore.com/tx/houston/945-mckinney/shredding), [all Houston stores](https://locations.theupsstore.com/tx/houston)). Call first.
- **Staples** lists in-store shredding by the pound but posts no price ([Staples](https://www.staples.com/stores/recycling)). Call your store.

## When you are cleaning out a lot of paper

Shred-it lists a drop-off at 10801 Kempwood Dr #4 at **$75 per 96-gallon tote**, which it says holds about 10 to 12 letter-size boxes. It's only open Mondays and Fridays, 9 to 11 a.m. and 1 to 4 p.m. You need an appointment, and you should prepay online. Cash isn't accepted ([Shred-it Houston](https://www.shredit.com/en-us/service-locations/houston)).
