San Antonio doesn't have a free weekday shred drop-off like some Texas cities, so most of the time you'll pay by the pound at a store. The cheapest posted price we found is Pak Mail on Broadway. For a big clean-out, a Shred-it tote is usually better value, and free shred days pop up in spring and fall.

## When you need it done this week

- **$1.20 a pound at Pak Mail.** Shred Nations lists Pak Mail at 7700 Broadway Ste 104 at $1.20 per pound, open weekdays 9 to 6 and Saturdays 10 to 2. It says prices aren't guaranteed ([Shred Nations San Antonio](https://www.shrednations.com/tx/san-antonio/)).
- **FedEx Office, $1.49 a pound.** San Antonio store pages list $1.49, including 2323 Babcock Rd, 4418 Broadway, 1275 NE Loop 410, 602 NW Loop 410, 13420 San Pedro Ave, 3103 SE Military Dr, La Cantera, Sonterra, and W Loop 1604 N ([2323 Babcock Rd](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/san-antonio/office-0127), [4418 Broadway](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/san-antonio/office-0135)). The downtown and resort hotel stores, including the Hyatt Regency on Losoya St, list $1.56 ([123 Losoya St](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/san-antonio/office-5577)). FedEx says staples, paper clips, rubber bands, and small binder clips can stay on ([FedEx Office shredding](https://www.office.fedex.com/default/shredding-services)).
- **The UPS Store.** Stores on E Basse Rd, Broadway in Alamo Heights, San Pedro Ave, Huebner Rd, Lockhill-Selma, Stone Oak Pkwy, Martin Luther King Dr, and at the convention center describe Iron Mountain drop-off: they weigh your paper, charge by the pound, and you put it in a locked bin. Their pages don't post a price ([999 E Basse Rd](https://locations.theupsstore.com/tx/san-antonio/999-e-basse-rd/shredding), [all San Antonio stores](https://locations.theupsstore.com/tx/san-antonio)). Call first.
- **Staples** lists in-store shredding by the pound but posts no price ([Staples](https://www.staples.com/stores/recycling)). Call your store.

## When you are cleaning out a lot of paper

Shred-it lists a drop-off at 7007 Fairgrounds Pkwy on the northwest side at **$75 per 96-gallon tote**, which it says holds about 10 to 12 letter-size boxes. It's open Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. You need an appointment, and you should prepay online. Cash isn't accepted ([Shred-it San Antonio](https://www.shredit.com/en-us/service-locations/san-antonio)). At FedEx's $1.49 a pound, a dozen boxes would cost far more.

## Shred day coming up

**Credit Human** lists its annual Shred Day for **Saturday, April 10, 2027, from 8 to 11 a.m.** at its Park Ten Financial Health Center, 6243 IH-10. Use the entrance on the I-10 frontage road. There's a limit of ten boxes per vehicle. Credit Human describes it as an event for members, so call to check before you go if you aren't one ([Credit Human events](https://credithuman.com/events/)).

## Shredding at home? Use the green cart, not the blue one

If you get City of San Antonio cart service, shredded paper goes in your **green organics cart**, loose or in a plain brown paper bag ([City of San Antonio organics](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/Recycling-Organics/Organics)). The **blue recycling cart does not take shredded paper**, and the city charges a fee on your CPS Energy bill for unaccepted items in a cart ([City of San Antonio recycling](https://www.sa.gov/Directory/Departments/SWMD/Recycling-Organics/Recycling)).
