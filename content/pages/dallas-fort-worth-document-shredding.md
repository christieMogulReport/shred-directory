Dallas-Fort Worth is big enough that your best option depends on which city you live in. Fort Worth residents can shred for free nearly any day. Everyone else in the metroplex has a free shred day coming up on December 5, a $10-a-box drop-off in Coppell, and per-pound shredding at UPS Store and FedEx Office.

## If you live in Fort Worth: free shredding most days

The City of Fort Worth has secure shred carts at all four of its Drop-Off Stations: 2400 Brennan Ave, 5150 Martin Luther King Fwy, 6260 Old Hemphill Rd, and 301 Hillshire Dr. Residents can bring up to three boxes per visit, at no more than 40 pounds each. Staples and paper clips can stay on, but metal clips, binders, CDs, photos, credit cards, and plastic can't go in. Junk mail and papers that show only a name and address go in recycling, not the shred cart. You need a recent Fort Worth water bill or a driver's license with a Fort Worth address, plus an active city sanitation account. The paper goes to the Tarrant County Secure Document Destruction facility, and you can't watch it being shredded ([Shred day is every day](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/shredday)).

The stations are open Tuesday to Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Saturday 8 a.m. to noon. They are closed Sunday, Monday, and city holidays, **including Thanksgiving (Nov 26) and the Friday after (Nov 27)** ([Drop-Off Stations](https://www.fortworthtexas.gov/departments/environmental-services/solidwaste/dropoff)). One city page says the limit is six boxes every 30 days and the other says six every 3 months, so call 311 at (817) 392-1234 if you have a lot.

## Free for anyone in DFW: Saturday, December 5, 2026

Mobile Shred It and United Electronic Recycling are holding **DFW Recycles Day** at 505 Airline Dr in Coppell from 9 a.m. to noon. Shredding is free for all DFW residents, so you don't need to live in Coppell. The event page caps paper at **two banker boxes or one tall kitchen trash bag per vehicle**. You stay in your car, and you can pick a 15-minute drop-off slot. Remove large binder clips, binders, coil binding, hanging folders, plastic bags, and magnets. Staples, paper clips, rubber bands, and file folders are fine ([Mobile Shred It event page](https://www.mobileshredit.com/community-event/dfw-recycles-day-free-paper-shredding-in-dfw-2026/), [United Electronic Recycling](https://unitedelectronicrecycling.com/dfw-recycles-day-all-you-need-to-know-2026/)). Mobile Shred It's general page mentions a four-box limit for its free days, but go by the two-box limit on the event page.

If you miss it, the same company runs drive-through shred events with the City of Plano that are open to everyone, at **$10 per banker box, cash or check only**. The next one is Saturday, December 19, 2026, 9 to 11 a.m. at Christ United Methodist Church, 3101 Coit Rd ([event page](https://www.mobileshredit.com/community-event/paper-shredding-near-plano-tx-12-19-26/)). United Electronic Recycling's calendar also lists paper shredding at St Andrew Methodist, 5801 W Plano Pkwy, on December 12, 2026 and January 9, 2027 ([UER events](https://unitedelectronicrecycling.com/community-events/)).

## When you have a few boxes and need it done now

- **$10 a box in Coppell.** Mobile Shred It says it usually charges $10 per banker box at its drop-off, 505 Airline Dr, open weekdays 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. ([Mobile Shred It](https://www.mobileshredit.com/free-shredding-for-dfw-residents/)). A full banker box often weighs more than 10 pounds, so this can beat per-pound pricing. Call (214) 337-3227 to confirm.
- **$1.29 a pound in Dallas.** Shred Nations lists Knox Street Postal Center, 2310 N Henderson Ave, as its Dallas drop-off and says the price isn't guaranteed ([Shred Nations Dallas](https://www.shrednations.com/dallas/)).
- **FedEx Office, $1.49 a pound.** Most Dallas store pages list this price, including Greenville Ave, W Northwest Hwy, Oak Lawn Ave, Dallas Pkwy, and N Stemmons Fwy. The downtown Olive St, Reunion Blvd, and N International Pkwy stores list $1.56 ([5500 Greenville Ave](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/dallas/office-0126), [400 Olive St](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/dallas/office-2814)).
- **The UPS Store.** Dallas stores on McKinney Ave, N Akard St, Lemmon Ave, E Mockingbird Ln, Greenville Ave, and elsewhere describe Iron Mountain drop-off: they weigh your paper, charge by the pound, and you put it in a locked bin. No prices are posted ([2633 McKinney Ave](https://locations.theupsstore.com/tx/dallas/2633-mckinney-ave/shredding), [all Dallas stores](https://locations.theupsstore.com/tx/dallas)). Call first.

## When you are cleaning out a lot of paper

Shred-it lists a drop-off at 420 Freeport Pkwy in Coppell at **$75 per 96-gallon tote**, which it says holds about 10 to 12 letter-size boxes. It's open weekdays 8:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m., you need an appointment, and you should prepay online. Cash isn't accepted ([Shred-it Dallas](https://www.shredit.com/en-us/service-locations/dallas)).

## Free events that have passed (watch for 2027)

- **City of Dallas:** Its Community Clean Trash-Off events used to include shredding, but the city now says "Document shredding is no longer offered" at them ([Keep Dallas Beautiful](https://dallascityhall.com/departments/codecompliance/keep-Dallas-beautiful/Pages/community-clean-trash-off.aspx)).
- **Mesquite Recycles Day** happens every May and November. The 2026 dates (May 16 and Nov 14) have passed. It's for residents only, and the shred truck takes personal documents only ([City of Mesquite](https://www.cityofmesquite.com/391/Mesquite-Recycles-Day)).
- **Garland Big 4 Recycle Day** was held on Nov 14, 2026, for residents only, two boxes each ([City of Garland](https://www.garlandtx.gov/m/calendar/event/detail/13467)).
- **AARP Texas** held free shred events in Dallas-Fort Worth on April 25, 2026 ([AARP Texas](https://www.aarp.org/states/texas/fraud-prevention-efforts/)).

Free events usually cap the number of boxes per car, run for just a few hours, and end early if the truck fills, so get there early.
