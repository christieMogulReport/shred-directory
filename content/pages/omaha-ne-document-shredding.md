Omaha doesn't have a standing free drop-off for household paper, so on a normal week you'll pay by the pound at a store. The lowest posted price we found is the UPS Store on W Dodge Rd at $1 a pound. For a big clean-out, a Shred-it tote in Bennington is usually better value, and the one big free shred day in town, Veridian's, comes once a year in September.

## When you need it done this week

- **$1 a pound on W Dodge Rd.** Shred Nations lists The UPS Store at 7914 W Dodge Rd at $1 per pound, open weekdays 8 to 7:30, Saturdays 9 to 5, and Sundays 10 to 3. It says prices aren't guaranteed ([Shred Nations Omaha](https://www.shrednations.com/ne/omaha/)). The store's number is (402) 397-0100.
- **FedEx Office, $1.49 a pound.** Omaha store pages list $1.49 at 209 S 72nd St, 740 N 114th St, 13003 W Center Rd, and 16811 Burke St ([209 S 72nd St](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/ne/omaha/office-1407), [740 N 114th St](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/ne/omaha/office-1410), [13003 W Center Rd](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/ne/omaha/office-1414), [16811 Burke St](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/ne/omaha/office-1848)). The convention-center store at 1001 Cass St lists $1.56 ([1001 Cass St](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/ne/omaha/office-5587)). FedEx says staples, paper clips, rubber bands, and small binder clips can stay on ([FedEx Office shredding](https://www.office.fedex.com/default/shredding-services)).
- **The UPS Store.** Stores on Birch Dr (Eagle Run Plaza), Weir St, W Center Rd (Lakeside Plaza), S 17th St downtown, N 156th St, S 84th St, N 114th St, S 204th St in Elkhorn, and Oakmont Dr near Gretna describe Iron Mountain drop-off: they weigh your paper, charge by the pound, and you put it in a locked bin. Their pages don't post a price ([4089 S 84th St](https://locations.theupsstore.com/ne/omaha/4089-s-84th-street/shredding), [all Omaha stores](https://locations.theupsstore.com/ne/omaha)). Call first.
- **Staples** lists in-store shredding by the pound but posts no price ([Staples](https://www.staples.com/stores/recycling)). Call your store.

## When you are cleaning out a lot of paper

Shred-it lists a drop-off at 13505 N 216th St in Bennington, on the northwest edge of the metro, at **$75 per 96-gallon tote**, which it says holds about 10 to 12 letter-size boxes. It's open Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. You need an appointment, and you should prepay online. Cash isn't accepted ([Shred-it Omaha](https://www.shredit.com/en-us/service-locations/omaha)). At FedEx's $1.49 a pound, a dozen boxes would cost far more.

## Free shred days

- **Veridian Credit Union** holds one free Community Shred Day in Omaha each year. In 2026 it was **Saturday, September 12, 9 a.m. to noon (past)**, at its support center, 810 N 96th St. It was open to the public, with a limit of two containers of up to 25 pounds each. You stay in your car and staff unload ([Veridian fall 2026 shred days](https://www.veridiancu.org/news/articles/fall-2026-community-shred-days)). Veridian says 2027 dates will be posted on [its shred page](https://www.veridiancu.org/shred), where you can sign up for an email alert.
- **Union Bank & Trust** lists a free Shred Day for **Saturday, April 17, 2027, 9 a.m. to noon**, with a 50-pound limit. It's at the **27th & Pine Lake branch in Lincoln**, not in Omaha. Don't arrive early: the lot won't be open and no waiting is allowed ([UBT Shred Day](https://www.ubt.com/learning-center/events/shred-day)). UBT's Omaha branch pages show the same event, so call (800) 297-2837 to ask whether an Omaha date is planned.

Free events usually cap how much you can bring, run for just a few hours, and can end early if the truck fills, so get there early.

## Shredding at home? Keep it out of the green-lid cart

The City of Omaha does **not** want shredded paper loose or bagged in your green-lid recycling cart. It slips through the sorting machines and ends up in the landfill. A handful sealed inside a paper envelope you were going to recycle anyway is OK. Put larger amounts in the trash, or take them to a shred event instead ([Omaha Recycling Guide: shredded paper](https://www.omaharecyclingguide.org/shredded-paper), [Wasteline Spring 2026](https://www.wasteline.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/WL-Spring-26.pdf)). The city's tip: tear off just the part with your name, address, and account number, shred that, and recycle the rest of the page whole.
