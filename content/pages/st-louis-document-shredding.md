St. Louis spans two states, and its shredding options do too. You can pay by the pound at a UPS Store, FedEx Office, or hardware store any day they are open, pay by the tote at a commercial drop-off for a big load, or wait for a free shred day from a city, a neighborhood group, or a bank.

## When you have a few boxes and need it done now

Pay-by-the-pound drop-off is the quickest route. The UPS Store on Laclede Ave in the Central West End says it works with Iron Mountain: your papers go on a scale, you pay a per-pound price, and you put them in a locked shredding container. It takes paper as is, with staples, paper clips, and folders, but not cardboard, phone books, hardcover books, CDs and DVDs, three-ring or lever-arch binders, hanging folders, transparencies, or large bulldog clips ([store page](https://locations.theupsstore.com/mo/saint-louis/4571-laclede-avenue/shredding)). The Hampton Village, Tesson Ferry, and Central City Plaza stores in St. Louis, and the Belleville store on the Illinois side, describe the same service. None of them post a price, so call first.

Shred Nations lists an **Ace Hardware at 11767 Manchester Rd** as its St. Louis drop-off at **$0.89 per pound**, the lowest per-pound price we found, and says the price isn't guaranteed and is set by the store ([Shred Nations St. Louis](https://www.shrednations.com/st-louis/)).

FedEx Office posts its price on each store page. Most St. Louis-area stores we checked list **$1.49 per pound**, including 6465 Forsyth Blvd, 8805 Ladue Rd, 11711 Manchester Rd, and 3112 S Grand Blvd ([Forsyth Blvd](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/mo/st-louis/office-5030), [S Grand Blvd](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/mo/st-louis/office-4701)). The two downtown stores, 800 Washington Ave and 315 Chestnut St, list **$1.56 per pound** ([Washington Ave](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/mo/st-louis/office-0199), [Chestnut St](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/mo/st-louis/office-5615)). FedEx says staples, paper clips, rubber bands, and small binder clips can stay on, but it won't take cardboard, books, 3-ring binders, hanging folders, or electronic media, and it shreds off-site after collecting paper in locked bins ([FedEx Office shredding](https://www.office.fedex.com/default/shredding-services)).

## When you are cleaning out a lot of paper

At $1 or more per pound, a big clean-out adds up fast. Shred-it lists a drop-off at 629 Lambert Pointe Dr in Hazelwood at $75 for a 96-gallon tote, which it says holds about 10 to 12 letter-size boxes. You need an appointment and should prepay online, since most locations can't take payment on site and cash isn't accepted ([Shred-it St. Louis](https://www.shredit.com/en-us/service-locations/st-louis)).

MRC, which runs it with the Soulard Community Improvement District, lists another on October 16, 2027, and says its events are open to the public ([MRC event page](https://mrcrecycling.net/electronics-recycling-events/soulard-community-document-04-17-2027)). The October 31, 2026 event took all kinds of documents, paper, credit cards, and bank statements ([Soulard CID](https://www.soulardcid.org/calendar/recycling1026)). Box limits for 2027 aren't posted, so call to confirm.
