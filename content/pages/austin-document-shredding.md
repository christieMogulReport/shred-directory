Austin is one of the easier cities for free shredding, because a recycling company runs a free weekday drop-off for residents. If you can get there on a weekday, that's the first place to try. For weekends or evenings, your options are a credit union shred day or a store that charges by the pound.

## Free shredding you can use any weekday

**Circular Services** runs a free Community Shred Drop Off at 9301 Johnny Morris Rd in northeast Austin, **Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 2 p.m.** You don't need an appointment, but you check in at reception. You can bring up to five bankers' boxes or bags per person of paper only, such as mail, statements, tax records, and medical paperwork. It's meant for residents, so businesses need to contact Circular Services instead, and you don't get a certificate of destruction ([Circular Services program terms](https://circularservices.com/mktg-shred-drop-off-terms-and-conditions/)). The City of Austin's shredding list shows this address under its older name, Balcones Shred ([Austin Resource Recovery](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/shredded-paper-drop-locations)).

## Shred day coming up

**United Heritage Credit Union** is holding a free shred day on **Saturday, December 12, 2026, from 9 to 11 a.m.** at its North Austin branch, 8715 W Parmer Ln. Members and the community can bring up to five file-size boxes for on-site shredding. Paper only, weather permitting ([UHCU event](https://www.uhcu.org/events/shred-day-parmer-north-austin-branch)). UHCU says its shred days end early if the truck fills up, so get there early ([UHCU Shred Days](https://www.uhcu.org/landing/shred-days)).

## When you need it done on a weekend or evening

- **$1 a pound at two UPS Stores.** Shred Nations lists The UPS Store at 1920 E Riverside Dr and at 3616 Far West Blvd at $1 per pound, and says prices aren't guaranteed ([Riverside](https://www.shrednations.com/tx/the-ups-store-19902-78741/), [Far West](https://www.shrednations.com/tx/the-ups-store-17986-78731/)).
- **FedEx Office, $1.49 a pound.** Austin store pages list $1.49, including 2711 Guadalupe St near UT, 6406 N I-35, 5601 Brodie Ln, 327 Congress Ave, 9222 Burnet Rd, and 600 E Ben White Blvd ([2711 Guadalupe](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/austin/office-0122), [5601 Brodie](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/austin/office-0723)). The downtown hotel stores at 304 E Cesar Chavez, 110 E 2nd St, and 101 Red River list $1.56 ([304 E Cesar Chavez](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/tx/austin/office-2873)). FedEx says staples, paper clips, and small binder clips can stay on ([FedEx Office shredding](https://www.office.fedex.com/default/shredding-services)).
- **The UPS Store.** Stores on E 6th St, E 41st St, W 6th St, W William Cannon Dr, and in the Hilton downtown describe Iron Mountain drop-off: they weigh your paper, charge by the pound, and you put it in a locked bin. Their pages don't post a price ([2401 E 6th St](https://locations.theupsstore.com/tx/austin/2401-e-6th-street/shredding)). Call first.
- **Office Depot and Staples.** Office Depot lists Iron Mountain shredding at Austin stores including 2101 S Lamar and 2620 W Anderson Ln ([Office Depot #304](https://www.officedepot.com/storelocator/tx/austin/office-depot-304/shredding-services)). Staples lists shredding by the pound ([Staples](https://www.staples.com/stores/recycling)). Neither posts a price, so call.

## When you are cleaning out a lot of paper

The free Circular Services drop-off caps you at five boxes, and you can't use it for business records. For more than that, Shred-it lists a drop-off at 4129 Commercial Center Dr in southeast Austin at **$75 per 96-gallon tote**, which it says holds about 10 to 12 letter-size boxes. It's open Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. You need an appointment, and you should prepay online. Cash isn't accepted ([Shred-it Austin](https://www.shredit.com/en-us/service-locations/austin)). The city's list also names local companies that shred for a fee, including A-Tex Shredding on Burnet Rd and ATI Secure Docs on Burleson Rd. Call them for prices ([Austin Resource Recovery](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/shredded-paper-drop-locations)).

## Shredding at home? Keep it out of the blue cart

Circular Services and Austin ARMA both say not to put shredded paper in your curbside recycling cart, because the pieces are too small to sort ([Circular Services](https://circularservices.com/mktg-shred-drop-off-terms-and-conditions/), [Austin ARMA](https://www.austinarma.com/shred-day)). Bag it and drop it off instead. Circular Services takes bagged shredded paper as long as it isn't mixed with unshredded paper. The city's Recycle and Reuse Drop-off Center, 2514 Business Center Dr, also takes shredded paper in paper bags, but you need an appointment (512-974-4343). A-Tex Shredding and Data Armor in Hutto take shredded paper for free ([Austin Resource Recovery](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/shredded-paper-drop-locations)).
