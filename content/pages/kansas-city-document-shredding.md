Kansas City straddles two states, and so do its shredding options. You can pay by the pound at a UPS Store or FedEx Office any day they are open, pay by the tote at a commercial drop-off for a big load, or wait for a free shred day from AARP, a city, a county sheriff, or a bank.

## When you have a few boxes and need it done now

Pay-by-the-pound drop-off is the quickest route. The UPS Store at 905 McGee St downtown says it works with Iron Mountain: your papers go on a scale, you pay a per-pound price, and you put them in a locked shredding container. It takes paper as is, with staples, paper clips, and folders, but not cardboard, phone books, hardcover books, CDs and DVDs, three-ring or lever-arch binders, hanging folders, transparencies, or large bulldog clips ([store page](https://locations.theupsstore.com/mo/kansas-city/905-mcgee-st/shredding)).

Shred Nations lists that same store as its Kansas City drop-off at $1.00 per pound and says the price isn't guaranteed. It says drop-off is the economical choice under about 3 boxes (roughly 90 pounds), and that drop-off points don't let you watch the shredding, don't give a certificate of destruction, and don't take hard drives or electronics ([Shred Nations Kansas City](https://www.shrednations.com/kansas-city/)).

FedEx Office's standard price is $1.49 per pound, and the downtown (1111 Main St) and Westport store pages both list that price. Two FedEx counters inside Northland Walmarts, on N Church Rd and N Boardwalk Ave, list **$1.09 per pound**, the lowest per-pound price we found in the metro ([N Church Rd](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/mo/kansas-city/office-0495), [N Boardwalk Ave](https://local.fedex.com/en-us/mo/kansas-city/office-2682)). FedEx says staples, paper clips, rubber bands, and small binder clips can stay on, but it won't take cardboard, books, 3-ring binders, hanging folders, or electronic media ([FedEx Office shredding](https://www.office.fedex.com/default/shredding-services)).

## When you are cleaning out a lot of paper

At $1 or more per pound, a big clean-out adds up fast. Shred-it lists a drop-off at 10000 Lackman Road in Lenexa at $75 for a 96-gallon tote, which it says holds about 10 to 12 letter-size boxes. You need an appointment and should prepay online, since most locations can't take payment on site and cash isn't accepted ([Shred-it Kansas City](https://www.shredit.com/en-us/service-locations/kansas-city)).
