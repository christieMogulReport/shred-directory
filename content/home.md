Old bank statements, tax papers, and pre-approved credit offers pile up fast, and the question is always the same: where can I get this shredded without paying a fortune or hunting for a business that is open?

These guides cover the three realistic options: **free community shred events** (credit unions, AARP chapters, libraries, and local governments host them), **drop-off shredding** at stores that charge by the pound, and **a home shredder** for small stacks. Each page links to its sources, and says plainly when something needs a phone call to confirm.

*General information only, not legal or tax advice. Hours, prices, and event dates change, so confirm with the business or host before you go.*
