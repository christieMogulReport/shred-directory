Where to shred documents in Oklahoma City, OK

Last updated November 18, 2026

You have two kinds of options in Oklahoma City. Stores take paper on any day they’re open and charge by the pound. Free community shred days happen a few times a year, and most are hosted by credit unions and local governments.

Coming up: free shredding in Moore on November 21

The City of Moore lists a Fall Recycle Event on Saturday, November 21, 2026, from 8 AM to noon at the Moore Recycle Center, 220 N Telephone Rd. It offers drive-thru paper shredding and electronics recycling (City of Moore). The page doesn’t list a box limit or say who can attend, so call 405-793-4373 before you go.

When you need it done this week

Pay-by-the-pound drop-off is the fastest option.

  • FedEx Office publishes a price: in-store shredding is $1.49 per pound at participating stores (FedEx Office shredding). The FedEx pages for four OKC stores list shredding at that price: 101 N Robinson Ave, 2601 W Memorial Rd, 7101 Northwest Expy, and 2205 SW 74th St. FedEx says staples, paper clips, rubber bands, and small binder clips can stay on. It won’t take binders, hanging folders, cardboard, books, or CDs.
  • The UPS Store works with Iron Mountain. Staff weigh your paper, charge a per-pound price, and you put it in a locked container. Staples and paper clips are fine, but the stores won’t take cardboard, phone books, hardcover books, CDs and DVDs, three-ring binders, hanging folders, transparencies, or large bulldog clips (UPS Store Mid-Town). Shred Nations lists the Mid-Town store at 4 NE 10th St as its OKC drop-off at $1.00 per pound and says the price isn’t guaranteed (Shred Nations Oklahoma City). Other UPS Stores set their own prices, so call first.
  • Staples charges per pound at the print center but doesn’t publish a rate. It won’t take binders, cardboard, hanging folders, large binder clips, ID badges, or discs (Staples recycling and shredding FAQ).

Shred Nations says that for more than about 3 boxes (roughly 90 pounds), mobile or offsite shredding is usually cheaper than drop-off. It also says you usually can’t watch your paper being shredded at a drop-off location (Shred Nations Oklahoma City).

Free events usually limit how many boxes you can bring and only run for a few hours, so arrive early. Also see how to shred documents for free.
