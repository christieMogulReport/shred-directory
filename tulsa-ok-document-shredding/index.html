Where to Shred Documents

Free shred days, drop-off shredding, and what is safe to toss.

Document shredding in Tulsa, OK

Last updated November 18, 2026

Tulsa has two kinds of options: stores that take paper any day they are open and charge by weight, and free community shred days that happen a few times a year.

When you need it done this week

Pay-by-the-pound drop-off is the fastest route. The UPS Store on S Mingo Road says it works with Iron Mountain, weighs your documents, charges a per-pound price, and has you place them in a locked shredding container. The store page says it will accept paper just as it is, with staples and paper clips, but not cardboard, phone books, hardcover books, CDs and DVDs, three-ring binders, hanging folders, transparencies, or large bulldog clips (store page).

Shred Nations lists a separate Tulsa drop-off at 1611 S Utica Ave at $1 per pound. It notes that drop-off locations don’t let you witness the shredding and can’t provide a certificate of destruction, and that over about 3 boxes (roughly 90 pounds) mobile or offsite shredding is usually more economical (Shred Nations Tulsa). Prices can change, so ask before you drop off.

When you can wait for a free event

Local credit unions have hosted free shred days in 2026, including Oklahoma Central Credit Union in Broken Arrow (April 25) and Red Crown Credit Union in Tulsa (September 2). Both dates have passed, so these are examples of what to watch for rather than upcoming events. AARP has also listed drive-through shred events in Tulsa on its events pages (AARP Events).

Free events usually have a limit on boxes per car and a short window, so arrive early.
